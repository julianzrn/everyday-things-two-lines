# 候选与筛选（u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479）

比赛：Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run

## 要求（据 make.fun 公开 description 与 requirements 核对）

严格两行。第一行用一个类比、一个生动的句子，向 10 岁孩子解释一件日常事物（烤面包机、堵车、雨、排队等）；第二行用一句话说清这个类比为什么成立：它映射了什么、又漏掉了什么。评审先看是否真的讲懂了，再看类比是否具体新奇、第二行是否诚实具体。description 未注明字符上限，两行均从严控制。

字符上限：未注明（两行各控制在 200 字符内）。字符数均为含标点、空格（两行条目含中间换行符）的实际字符数，由脚本逐条核对。

## 候选（共 8 条）

1.（377 字符）A queue is a human train where every person is a carriage: it only moves when the front carriage moves, and nobody can unhook and rush ahead without breaking the whole train.
   The train works because each of us, like a carriage, rolls forward only when the gap ahead opens — but it leaves out that carriages never get bored, check their phones, or quietly leave to buy a coffee.
   简评：排队=人类火车：孩子一听就懂「前面不动我不动」；第二行点名漏掉的是人的情绪与手机，诚实且具体。

2.（367 字符）A traffic jam is a river of cars that forgot how to flow: one driver slows down to look at something, and the slowdown ripples backward until the whole river is standing still.
   It works because cars bunch up behind any sudden narrowing or pause, just like water — but it leaves out that water never honks, changes its mind, or causes the very blockage it is stuck in.
   简评：堵车=停滞的河，波纹回传的意象准确；第二行用「水不会按喇叭」点漏，幽默但略绕。

3.（355 字符）Rain is the sky's sponge getting squeezed: clouds soak up tiny drops of water until they are too heavy to hold them, and then everything drips and pours down on us.
   It maps how water gathers up high and falls once there is too much of it, but it leaves out that nobody does the squeezing — the drops grow fat by bumping into each other until gravity wins.
   简评：雨=被挤的海绵，第一行好懂；第二行自曝「没人挤」并给出真实机制（碰撞长大），诚实分高，但句子偏长。

4.（377 字符）Wi-Fi is invisible shouting between your tablet and a small box in the hallway: the box yells the internet in a secret code, and every wall it shouts through makes the words fainter.
   It works because the signal really does travel through the air and weaken with distance and walls, but it leaves out that a shouting box never gets hoarse — it repeats itself perfectly, forever.
   简评：Wi-Fi=隔空喊话，衰减讲得直观；第二行「不会嗓子哑」点漏巧妙，惜类比本身较常见。

5.（310 字符）A toaster is a tiny sunbathing bed for bread: two slices lie under glowing orange bars until their pale sides turn golden, crisp, and ready for butter.
   It maps how steady heat browns the outside while the middle stays soft, but it leaves out that the sun never pops your breakfast up and dings when it is done.
   简评：烤面包机=日光浴床，画面鲜活；第二行点漏（太阳不会弹起叮一声）可爱，但「为什么变金黄」讲得浅。

6.（377 字符）An escalator is a staircase that does the climbing for you: a loop of steps rolls up like a conveyor belt, carries you to the top, then folds flat and sneaks back down underneath.
   It works because the steps really do move in an endless loop, but it leaves out that a belt never gets tired — and that the steps are only pretending to be stairs while the motor does all the work.
   简评：扶梯=会动的楼梯，循环讲清了；第二行点漏落在电机，偏机械说明，惊奇感一般。

7.（360 字符）A fridge is a winter that lives in a box in your kitchen: it keeps blowing cold air in circles so your milk believes it is January and stays fresh for weeks.
   It maps how cold slows everything down, including the tiny germs that spoil food, but it leaves out that real winter comes free — this one hums, leaks heat out the back, and raises the electricity bill.
   简评：冰箱=盒子里的冬天，「牛奶以为是一月」很孩子气；第二行信息量大但稍散。

8.（361 字符）An elevator is a small room that climbs ropes inside a tall building, stopping at floors the way a bus stops at stations, except it only ever travels up and down one street.
   It works because the room is pulled along a fixed route and opens only at set stops, but it leaves out that buses have drivers — the elevator just trusts its buttons, its cables, and you.
   简评：电梯=竖直的公交车，类比工整；第二行点漏（没有司机）到位，但整体惊喜度不如排队条。

## 定稿

**A queue is a human train where every person is a carriage: it only moves when the front carriage moves, and nobody can unhook and rush ahead without breaking the whole train.
The train works because each of us, like a carriage, rolls forward only when the gap ahead opens — but it leaves out that carriages never get bored, check their phones, or quietly leave to buy a coffee.**（377 字符）

选定理由：八组里排队这组最贴评审三维度：第一行类比一秒讲懂（车厢只能等前面动），且自带规则感（脱钩就散架）；第二行先点映射（间隙决定滚动），再点漏掉的部分时不泛泛说「人有感情」，而是落到三个具体动作——无聊、刷手机、溜去买咖啡，诚实且有画面。语言对 10 岁孩子无门槛。
